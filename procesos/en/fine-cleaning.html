Fine Cleaning

Seed is calibrated by size and density. Without that uniformity, the drum coating won't adhere evenly.

Cleaning area Floor operator ~12 min per lot Calibration

In simple

Seed leaves this step calibrated and free of foreign matter. That's what makes the drum coating uniform — and what gives every pellet the same size.

In detail

The operator runs two coupled passes: first a density table that separates by weight (filled seeds vs empty ones), then a sieve classifier that separates by size. The calibrated seed has a tight size distribution. The system logs the final purity and moisture for audit; without this step registered, the drum refuses to load the lot.
