Finished Product Warehouse

Certified bags wait here until distribution. The inventory stays in real time and reorder alerts fire automatically.

Finished product warehouse Warehouse operator Continuous Product exit

In simple

Certified bags are stored until they leave. Every outbound move is registered — a lot is never lost from view once it leaves the lab.

In detail

The warehouse operator records each inbound and each outbound against the catalogue of certified lots. The system keeps an ACID transactional inventory: an outbound move discounts stock and simultaneously logs client, date and origin lot. When a lot falls below the reorder point, the system fires an asynchronous alert to the logistics dashboard — without need for manual polling.

Pipeline close

The finished-product warehouse is not just the last production link — it is the point of contact with the client. Every bag that leaves carries a complete identity document: the lot code, the rebuildable genealogy tree, the quality certificate and the digital signature of the operator who packaged it. That chain — from the raw-materials warehouse to the final warehouse, through the drum — is what makes the institute auditable before FAO, ISTA and ISO 9001.
