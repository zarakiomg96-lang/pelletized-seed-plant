Pre-cleaning

Before fine treatment, large impurities and damaged seeds are separated. The work is mechanical, but it leaves the lot's first discard record.

Cleaning area Floor operator ~10 min per lot Mechanical

In simple

Raw seed arrives with dust, leaves, soil clods and damaged seeds. Pre-cleaning separates those by size and weight before the seed goes on to fine treatment.

In detail

The operator feeds the lot into a vibrating screen combined with an air column: light particles are removed by suction, heavier material falls onto a second vibrating stage where soil clods and damaged seeds are caught. The system logs the discard percentage and the pass-through time; every kilo that leaves the flow is associated with its origin lot, so the discard itself is traceable backward.
