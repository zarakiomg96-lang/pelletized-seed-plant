Process 06 / 07 · decision node

Quality Control

The lab samples the lot and measures germination, physical purity and final moisture. The decision that leaves here is binding: it defines whether the lot is certified or routed to rework or discard.

Quality lab Specialist Sampling + assay Binding decision

In simple

The lab analyses samples from each lot. If germination, purity and moisture fall within certified ranges, the lot is signed off and moves to the warehouse. If not, it is flagged for rework or discard.

In detail

The specialist prepares three parallel trays from the lot: one for the germination assay in a humidity chamber over 7 days, one for physical purity by mechanical separation, and one for final moisture in a drying oven. The results are checked against FAO, ISTA and ISO 9001:2015 thresholds. If everything passes, a digital certificate is issued; if it fails, the lot bifurcates to rework (back to fine cleaning) or to discard depending on severity. Each assay is archived for years as auditable evidence.

Why this step is the decision node

Quality control closes the algorithmic cycle. The system does not allow a lot flagged for rework to reach the finished-product warehouse, nor does it allow an approved lot to return to the flow. State transitions are validated by two parallel fronts: the lab result and the database's ACID guarantees. If a network failure prevents the COMMIT in PostgreSQL, the entire lot reverts to its previous state — without leaving ghost records.
