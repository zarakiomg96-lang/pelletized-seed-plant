Intake of Inputs and Base Seed

Every sack of raw seed and every input that enters the plant becomes a lot in the system. It is the zero-kilometre point of the traceability graph: everything that happens afterwards is chained back to this node.

Raw Materials Warehouse Warehouse operator Continuous Graph origin

In simple

When raw seed and inputs arrive at the warehouse, every sack is given a unique code and tied to its supplier, its date and its weight. From here on, the system can rebuild the lot's entire lineage — forward and backward.

In detail

The warehouse operator records each shipment of raw base seed and each input (polymers, fungicides, inert clay) with three immutable attributes: a lot code, a reception date and a supplier. The inventory unit is not the bag nor the kilogram — it is the lot. Every later transformation is logged as a directed edge that points back to this node, so the full lineage can be reconstructed with a single structured SQL statement.
